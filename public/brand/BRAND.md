# VANTA brand kit

**Give it a job. Keep the receipts.**

**Active X account:** <https://x.com/vantanss7>

VANTA is an observable autonomous work agent. The identity is an operator's field manual: bone paper, ink rules,
cobalt authorization and safety-orange stamps. The receipt is the recurring object; there is no robot, no gradient
mesh and no stock imagery.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bone paper | `#EDE7DB` | Default field |
| Cream stock | `#F7F3EA` | Receipts, cards |
| Ink | `#14161A` | Rules, primary type |
| Cobalt | `#1B2BE0` | Authorization, links, primary action |
| Signal | `#FF4D12` | Stamps, attention, single accent |
| Muted ink | `#6A6459` | Metadata, secondary copy |

Use flat fills. Do not add gradients, glows, rounded corners or drop shadows to the mark. Paper surfaces may use a
subtle grid; the cobalt field may use a cream grid.

## Typography

- **Display:** Bricolage Grotesque, 700–800, tight tracking (`-0.04em` to `-0.055em`).
- **Body:** Public Sans, 400–600.
- **Data / labels:** IBM Plex Mono, 400–600, uppercase, generous tracking.

If Bricolage is unavailable, use a heavy grotesque (Arial Black / Helvetica Bold) rather than a geometric or
serif substitute. Never letterspace the display headline loosely.

## Voice

- The promise is exact: **“Give it a job. Keep the receipts.”**
- Prefer concrete nouns: plan, tool, trace, source, approval, receipt, testnet USDC.
- State limitations plainly. “Testnet only” and “nothing runs until you decide” are part of the identity.
- Do not claim a mainnet transaction, a hosted model, or a capability that is not in the repository.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least the orange tab's width.
- Minimum mark size: 24 px digital. Below that, use the profile picture or a cobalt square with the V.
- Pair the mark with the wordmark when space allows; the V alone is the fallback.
- Signal orange is a stamp, rule or single accent — never a full background wash.
- Do not outline, crop, rotate or recolor the V. Do not place the mark on a busy photograph.

Accurate coverage, reviews and challenge submissions may use these assets. Do not imply endorsement, and do not use
the mark for a competing product. VANTA is a working product name; domain and trademark availability have not been
verified.
